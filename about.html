About Hereafter

A Place to Pause, Connect, and Stay Awhile

Hereafter Cafe was created with one simple idea: everyone deserves a place to slow down, enjoy good coffee, and feel at home.

Located along Luna Street in Iloilo City, Hereafter Cafe is surrounded by schools, local businesses, the public market, and the church—making it an accessible spot for students, friends, workers, and casual coffee-goers.

Whether you're taking a quick coffee break, catching up with friends, or spending hours working on your next assignment, Hereafter Cafe offers a comfortable space for you. Our second-floor co-working area provides an environment where ideas can flow and productivity can happen.

At Hereafter, we believe that sometimes, the best moments happen when you simply stay awhile.

Our Mission

To offer affordable and enjoyable coffee while providing a comfortable space where people can study, work, connect, and unwind.

Our Vision

To be a welcoming part of the Iloilo community—a café where everyone can enjoy good coffee and meaningful moments.

What We Value

  • Comfort – A space where you can relax and be yourself.
  • Affordability – Good coffee should be accessible to everyone.
  • Community – Bringing people together, one cup at a time.
  • Quality – Making every drink and every visit worth coming back for.
